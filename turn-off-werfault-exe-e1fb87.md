First, navigate to the Control Panel and select "System and Security," then click on "Action Center," and finally, access "Problem Reporting Settings" to choose "Never check for solutions" or "Automatically check for solutions" based on your preference. Alternatively, for a more advanced method, you can open the Registry Editor by typing "regedit" in the Run dialog (Win + R), then navigate to HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Windows\Windows Error Reporting and create a new DWORD (32-bit) value named "Disable WerFault" and set its value to 1. After making these changes, you may need to restart your computer for them to take effect. Disabling werfault.exe can help improve system performance and reduce unwanted pop-ups related to error reporting, but be cautious as it may also prevent useful feedback from being sent to Microsoft, which could lead to better software improvements in the future.
